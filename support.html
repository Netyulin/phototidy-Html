About the App

PhotoTidy is a local photo management tool that helps you organize, delete, favorite, and group photos by location. All operations run locally on your device to protect your privacy.

Features

- Gesture-based photo management
- Location-based photo grouping
- iCloud sync support
- In-app purchase to unlock premium features

Privacy & Security

We do not collect or upload any of your photos or personal data. All data processing happens locally on your device.

Troubleshooting

If you experience crashes, errors, or sync issues, please try restarting the app or your device. Ensure your system is updated to the latest version.
